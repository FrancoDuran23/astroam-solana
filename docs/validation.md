## 1. Interview Protocol & Questions

Conversations were conducted with travelers who visited neighboring countries (Bolivia, Chile, Brazil, Dominican Republic). The 4 questions asked:
1. **Destination & Connection:** Where did you travel, for how long, and how did you connect to mobile data?
2. **Spend & Unused Gigabytes:** How much did you pay for connectivity, and how much unused data was wasted or unspent?
3. **Crypto / Digital Dollars:** Do you hold USDC, USDT, or digital dollars, and in what wallet/app (Lemon, Belo, Binance, Phantom, etc.)?
4. **Product Feedback & Perception:** What is your opinion on AstroAm's model (pay only for used megabytes in digital dollars with automatic escrow refund)?

## 3. Key Takeaway & Evidence Line for Pitch Video

*Key synthesis delivered to Ignacio for the evidence block in the pitch video:*
> **"Interviews with 10 Argentine travelers show 70% resort to Wi-Fi hunting or waste over half of fixed roaming packs, while 40% already hold digital dollars (Lemon, Belo, Binance) and demand frictionless pay-as-you-go connectivity."**

### Core Insights:
1. **The Fear of Abusive Roaming:** Most travelers from Jujuy going to Bolivia/Chile intentionally avoid buying carrier packs because they feel ripped off, relying precariously on Wi-Fi or hassle-heavy local physical SIM cards.
2. **Leftover Waste:** Travelers who do buy fixed packages (Facundo, Gabriel, Matías) systematically leave unused gigabytes on the table (up to 60% wasted).
3. **Crypto Reality in Argentina:** Adoption is driven by consumer fintechs (Lemon, Belo, Binance). Users hold digital balances and are eager to spend them directly to dodge card taxes (30% perception).
