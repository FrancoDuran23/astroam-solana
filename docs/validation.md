# Validation

**Date:** 2026-10-07 · **Status:** one interview, and it is with the founder. Demand is not validated.

A founder describing his own trip is a starting point, not evidence that other people will pay. The interviews that count are the ones below the script, and they have not been done.

## Interview 1: Franco Durán, founder, answering as a traveler

2026-10-07. Argentine, holds stablecoins, travels to neighboring countries. He picked each answer from a short list of options, so these are not his words.

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Where was your last trip abroad? | A neighboring country. |
| How did you get mobile data? | Roaming from my Argentine carrier. |
| What did you pay in total? | Between US$15 and US$30. |
| How did you pay? | With an Argentine card. |
| How much of what you paid for did you use? | About half. |
| What bothered you most? | The card surcharges, losing what I did not use, and that it was complicated to activate. |
| Do you hold stablecoins, and would you pay for travel data with them? | Yes, and yes. |

What it suggests, to test with other people:

- The traveler paid for roughly twice the data he used. Paying only for usage would have cut that bill by about half.
- The card surcharge is felt as a separate cost, on top of the data.
- Activation was a problem with the carrier too. An eSIM that is hard to install would not fix that.

## Script for the next interviews

Ten minutes each. Ask about the last real trip, not about what they would do.

1. Where was your last trip abroad, and how long?
2. How did you get mobile data there? Why that way?
3. What did you pay, and how? Did the card add anything?
4. Did you use everything you paid for? What happened to the rest?
5. Do you hold USDC or another stablecoin? Where? Have you ever paid for something with it?

Close by showing the app for one minute and asking one thing: what would stop you from using this on your next trip?

## Who to interview

Five to ten Argentine travelers who hold USDC and have traveled abroad in the last year. The first place to look is where the team already has reach: jujuy.dev and Superteam Argentina.
