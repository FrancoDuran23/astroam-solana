# Validation

**Date:** October 2026 · **Status:** Desk research and price comparison only. Customer interviews are planned and have not been done. Demand is not validated. There is no paying user.

## What has been checked

Validation so far is desk research and a price comparison. No customer interview has been conducted. No quote, usage share, or willingness-to-pay figure in this repo comes from a traveler.

The comparison is published carrier and eSIM rates, read on 7–8 October 2026, set against AstroAm's pay-per-MB rate (reseller cost × 1.5). It lives in [go-to-market.md](go-to-market.md) and in the README under Go-to-market and validation. It is not evidence that travelers will buy.

That comparison does not say what share of a deposit comes back, whether travelers will pay in USDC, or what it costs to acquire one. Those are open. The same gaps are listed under "What we do not know" in [go-to-market.md](go-to-market.md).
